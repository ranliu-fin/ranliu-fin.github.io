<h2 style="margin: 60px 0px 10px;">Teaching</h2>

I have worked with MBA, EMBA, MSc, and PhD students at Columbia Business School in corporate finance, continuous-time finance, microeconomics, game theory, and computer programming, and undergraduate students at the University of International Business and Economics in banking.

My teaching interests include microeconomics and finance at all levels. Besides regular courses, I am happy to design and teach a course on Information in Financial Economics.
