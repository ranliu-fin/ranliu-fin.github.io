Ran Liu is a recent graduate of [Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/).

Her research interests lie at the intersection of **asset pricing**, **information economics**, **market microstructure**, and **behavioral finance**. Specifically, she studies the informational efficiency in financial markets: how investors acquire, process, and perceive information, and how these choices shape prices, portfolios, and welfare. Her work is primarily theoretical, with empirical and institutional applications that discipline the models.

Before Columbia, she received a Master's degree in Economics and Finance (with Distinction) from [Centro Estudios Monetarios y Financieros (CEMFI)](https://www.cemfi.es/), as well as Bachelor's degrees in Economics (Financial Engineering, with Honors) and Management (Information Management and Information Systems) from the [University of International Business and Economics (UIBE)](http://english.uibe.edu.cn/).
