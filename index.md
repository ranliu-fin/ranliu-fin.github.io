I am a recent graduate of [Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **asset pricing**, **information economics**, **market microstructure**, and **behavioral finance**. Specifically, I study the informational efficiency of financial markets: how investors acquire, process, and perceive information, and how these choices shape prices, portfolios, and welfare. My work is primarily theoretical, with empirical and institutional applications that discipline the models.

Before Columbia, I received a Master's degree in Economics and Finance (with Distinction) from [Centro Estudios Monetarios y Financieros (CEMFI)](https://www.cemfi.es/), as well as Bachelor's degrees in Economics (Financial Engineering, with Honors) and Management (Information Management and Information Systems) from the [University of International Business and Economics (UIBE)](http://english.uibe.edu.cn/).
